Delete your Zamu account

Zamu app, published by the Zamu team. Last updated: October 22, 2026

Translation provided for convenience. In case of difference, the French version prevails.

You can delete your Zamu account and the associated data at any time, directly from the app or by writing to us.

Method 1: from the app

  1. Open the Zamu app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab, then open Settings.
  3. At the bottom of the page, tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is hidden immediately (it no longer appears to other users), then permanently deleted with your data after 30 days. During these 30 days, you can cancel the deletion by signing in again.

Method 2: by email

If you no longer have access to the app:

  1. Write to support.zamuapp@gmail.com, subject "Account deletion request".
  2. Give the email address or username of your Zamu account, and write from that email address if possible.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days at most and confirm the deletion by email.

Data deleted

  • Profile: name, username, date of birth, gender, bio, interests, preferences
  • Profile and gallery photos
  • Messages, photos, videos and voice messages
  • Stories, likes, matches and follows
  • Geographic position
  • Login details (email, phone number, linked Google account) and notification token

Data kept

  • Reports about you: reports made by other users about you may be kept for up to 12 months, for users' safety and, if necessary, to answer requests from the authorities. Reports you made are deleted with your account.
  • Moderation record: if your account was subject to a safety alert or a ban, a dated record of the decision (identifier, name, email, declared date of birth, reason) is kept for 5 years, to prove compliance with our legal obligations, in particular the protection of minors.
  • Purchases: the payment history of the Premium subscription is kept by Google Play according to its own rules.
Premium subscription: deleting your account does not stop a current subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Profile > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions).

Delete part of your data without deleting your account

You can erase some data at any time, directly in the app, while keeping your account:

  • Profile and gallery photos: Profile > Edit, then delete the photo.
  • Stories: open your story, then Delete. They are also erased automatically when they expire.
  • Private album photos: Profile > My private album, then delete the photo.
  • Messages: long press on a sent message, then Delete. Read messages are also erased automatically after 3 days, except those you chose to keep (long press, then "Stop keeping" so they are erased).
  • Profile information (bio, height, weight, interests, "Available" status): Profile > Edit, then clear the field.
  • Location: remove the location permission in your phone settings, and turn off "Show my distance" in Zamu Settings.

For any other data, write to us at support.zamuapp@gmail.com saying what you want to delete: we do it within 30 days at most.
