Zamu Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Translation provided for convenience. In case of difference, the French version prevails.

This privacy policy describes how the Zamu application (the "app"), published by the Zamu team ("we"), collects, uses and protects the information of its users ("you"). It complements our Terms of Use.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account information: email address, phone number (if you sign in by phone), username, first name, date of birth, gender, information provided at sign-up or through Google sign-in.
  • Profile information: photos, bio, interests, height, weight and dating preferences, and, if you say so, what you are looking for on Zamu (serious relationship, we'll see, friendship), shown on your profile. The gender of the people you are looking for is for your own use only: it is never shown or passed on to other users.
  • Verification selfie (optional): if you request the "Verified profile" badge, you take a selfie copying a pose. It is seen only by the Zamu team, who compare it with your profile photos to fight fake profiles. It is never shown on your profile, and it is deleted as soon as the verification is approved or refused. No automatic facial recognition is used.
  • Story face effects (optional): if you add an effect (glasses, crown…) to a story photo, the app finds the position of the face directly on your phone (Google ML Kit). Nothing is sent or kept: only the photo you post, with the effect, is saved. This is not facial recognition (no one is identified).
  • Content: photos, videos, stories (with their texts, stickers and GIFs), text and audio messages sent in the app, group chat messages and photos, private album photos, gifts sent in messages (animations, gifted Premium), likes, favorites and event suggestions.
  • "Zamu Star" request (optional): if you request this status, the chosen category, your introduction, the link you give and your consent for your stories to be public. To check the criteria, the server counts your stories and their views over the last 30 days (a number, without their content); this count is erased after 31 days.
  • Connections: your friends list and friend requests, your "Close friends" and "Hide my story from" lists, your groups, and the events you say you are attending ("I'm going", "Interested").
  • Camera and microphone: used only when you take a photo or video, record a voice message or make a voice or video call in the app. We do not access all your photos: you choose the media you share.
  • Location data: geographic position, used to show nearby profiles and events, the distance between users and, if you tap "I'm here", to check that you are really at an event. Location is only used while the app is open. You can refuse or withdraw this permission in your phone settings.
  • Voice and video calls: only possible between two people who have matched, who are friends on Zamu, or who have each already exchanged several messages. Sound and video go directly from one phone to the other: they are never recorded or kept by Zamu. To set up the connection, the two phones exchange their network addresses (IP address), in particular through a Google connection server (STUN). We only keep the call log (who called whom, voice or video, date, duration, missed or declined call), and a "📞 Call" line in the conversation. You can refuse all calls in the settings ("Receive calls").
  • Activity data: last login ("online" status), profile and story views, reports and blocks.
  • Technical data: device model, system and app version, push notification identifier, error logs.
  • Bug reports (optional): if you use "Report a bug", your description, the app version, your phone model and Android version. They are used only to fix the problem and reply to you.
  • Subscription and purchases: status of your Premium subscription and Boosts, and the days of Premium given to or received from another member. We never have access to your banking details, as payment is handled by Google Play.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account, check that you are at least 18
  • Suggest relevant profiles and content, in particular based on your location
  • Allow messages, photos, videos and stories to be exchanged, between two people or in groups
  • Manage your friends, groups and participation in events
  • Allow voice and video calls between people who have matched, are friends or have already exchanged several messages, and alert you to an incoming call (ringtone, even when the app is closed)
  • Send you notifications (new messages, calls, likes, matches, friend requests, event reminders, activity summary)
  • Moderate content: automatic analysis of photos to detect nudity and explicit content, handling of reports
  • Protect members: texts (messages, group chats, bio, name, username) are analyzed automatically using a list of keywords, to spot people under 18 and dangerous messages (threats, distress, weapons, blackmail). Only the flagged texts, with the few messages around them, are passed to the Zamu team for review; no decision is taken automatically. For your safety, phone numbers and WhatsApp or Telegram links written in public texts (bio, name, status, story caption, group or event descriptions) are automatically hidden.
  • Manage your Premium subscription
  • Suggest the best time for a Boost, based on anonymous averages of the number of people online each hour (no personal data)
  • Keep the app secure, fight abuse and fake profiles
  • Comply with our legal obligations

3. What other users see

Your profile (first name, age, photos, bio, interests), your username, your approximate distance, your "online" status and your public stories are visible to other users. Your "Friends" or "Close friends" stories are only visible to the people concerned. Your exact location, email and phone number are never shown. Messages are only visible to their recipients; group messages, to the group members. Exception: a reported message, or one automatically flagged as dangerous or indicating an age under 18, may be read by the Zamu team together with the few messages around it. The list of people attending an event is only visible to other attendees. You can turn off search by username and friend requests in the settings. If you become a "Zamu Star", your ⭐ badge, category, introduction and link are visible to everyone, and your public stories are highlighted to all users.

4. Sharing information

We do not sell your personal data. It is shared only with the technical providers needed to run the app, strictly within the scope of their task:

  • Supabase: hosting of the database, files and accounts;
  • Google Firebase: sending push notifications;
  • Google (STUN server): helps connect the two phones during a call (network addresses only, never sound or video);
  • Google: sign-in with a Google account (if you choose it);
  • Sightengine: automatic analysis of posted photos, to detect nudity and explicit content;
  • RevenueCat and Google Play: management and payment of the Premium subscription;
  • OpenStreetMap: city search on the map (only the searched text is sent);
  • GIPHY: GIF and sticker search (only the searched text is sent).

These providers may process data outside Côte d'Ivoire. We may also disclose information to the competent authorities when required by law.

5. Data retention

  • Messages: deleted automatically 3 days after being read, including the photos, videos and audio they contain. A message you choose to keep ("Keep message") stays visible to both of you until it is no longer kept or the conversation or account is deleted.
  • Call log: deleted automatically after 30 days. The sound and video of calls are never recorded.
  • Stories: deleted automatically when they expire.
  • Group chats: messages and photos deleted automatically after 7 days; an event chat is deleted 3 days after the event ends.
  • Profile and photos: kept as long as your account is active.
  • Verification selfie: deleted as soon as the team decides (approved or refused). Only the result (verified profile or not) is kept, with the history of decisions (date and reason).
  • Safety alerts: the flagged text and the few messages around it are kept for up to 12 months, for members' safety and to meet our legal obligations, then deleted.
  • Moderation record: in case of an alert or a ban (in particular of a person under 18), we keep a dated record of the decision: account identifier and name, email address, declared date of birth, sentence concerned and reason. This record is used only to prove compliance with our rules and legal obligations (protection of minors); it is kept for 5 years, including after the account is deleted, then erased.
  • Account deletion: from the app settings, you can delete your account at any time. Your profile, photos, messages and stories are then erased. Some information related to reports may be kept as long as necessary for users' safety or to meet our legal obligations.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data (encrypted connections, access control to the database). As no system is completely secure, we encourage you never to share sensitive information, especially banking information, with other users.

7. Your rights

Under Ivorian law no. 2013-450 of June 19, 2013 on the protection of personal data, and according to the laws of your country of residence, you have the right to access, correct, object to and delete your data. You can change most of your information directly in the app, or write to us at the address below. You can also contact the data protection authority of your country (in Côte d'Ivoire: ARTCI).

8. Intended audience

Zamu is only for people aged 18 or over. We delete any account we learn belongs to a minor.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy. Any significant change will be notified to you through the app.
